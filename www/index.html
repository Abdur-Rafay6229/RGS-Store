Advanced (optional) — photo delete ke liye

Yeh dono field sirf isi account ke liye rehti hain (backup export me nahi jaati). Tabhi bharo jab bill delete hone par Cloudinary se photo bhi hatani ho. Sirf upload ke liye Cloud Name + Preset kaafi hain.
